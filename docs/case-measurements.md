# Kindle measurement audit

Checked 2026-10-07 through 2026-10-08. All dimensions are millimetres in height x width x maximum thickness order. These are nominal envelopes, not manufacturing drawings or verified physical fit.

Manufacturer means an Amazon publication or an Amazon-authored guide (mirrored copies are identified). Reference means dimensions retained from the reference catalogue pending a manufacturer or caliper check. Corner radii, openings and rear contours remain estimates except for the scan-informed Paperwhite 10 cavity.

38 profiles: 36 manufacturer-sourced envelopes and two reference envelopes (Kindle 2016 and Oasis 2016). Storage, Wi-Fi/cellular and Kids bundles may share a body, but verify the bare device; bundled covers are excluded.

## Standard shell

Every exterior uses vertical walls, a 0.4 mm bed chamfer at 45 degrees and a 0.5 mm rounded upper rim. Optional rear sections affect the cavity only. Default per-side clearance is 0.35 mm, independently adjustable per profile; base 2.4 mm and nominal wall 1.6 mm. Open edges have no retaining hooks. Lip slopes and above-bed downward faces are checked against 45 degrees in the serialized plain STL.

Oasis and original Kindle are conservative envelope trays without snap lips. They need a measured contour or insert/padding for close support; scans are optional. Voyage has a broad estimated back power-button window. Legacy speaker windows are estimates. The Scribe profiles leave side edges open for ports and pen attachment.

The plain case can be printed back-down. Inlaid pockets, relief textures, material settings and physical fit still need slicer and print checks.

The six October 2026 variants use published product specifications, with broad bottom/top/right openings and smaller 4 mm corner guards for the relocated upper-right power button. Actual access and retention must be checked. Signature Edition pogo-pin contact locations, charging docks and magnetic covers have not been measured; remove the case to use those accessories.
